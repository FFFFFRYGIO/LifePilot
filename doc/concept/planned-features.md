## Household Shopping Management

Track recurring household shopping needs across categories:

- Food
- Drinks
- Alcohol
- Household supplies
- Hygiene and personal care products
- Cleaning products
- Pet supplies
- Medicine and first-aid items

Capabilities:

- Manage products that are already bought, running low, or missing
- Create reusable shopping categories and custom product groups
- Generate shopping lists automatically based on missing or low-stock items
- Support big shopping plans for clothes, electronics, furniture, appliances, and other larger purchases

## Pantry and Home Inventory

- Track current household inventory
- Monitor what products are available at home
- Mark items as used, finished, expired, or missing
- Track quantities, storage location, and purchase date
- Monitor food expiration dates and estimated spoilage time
- Send reminders before food expires
- Help reduce food waste by highlighting products that should be used soon

## Chores and Home Maintenance

Manage recurring household chores such as:

- Cleaning
- Laundry
- Washing dishes
- Taking out trash
- Changing bedsheets
- Watering plants
- General home maintenance

Capabilities:

- Set reminders for daily, weekly, monthly, and custom schedules
- Track completed and overdue chores
- Assign chores to household members
- Monitor recurring maintenance tasks, such as replacing filters, checking appliances, or restocking supplies

## Personal Tasks and Responsibilities

- Manage private tasks and to-dos
- Track formal and administrative duties, such as documents, bills, taxes, appointments, and deadlines
- Organize tasks by priority, deadline, category, and status
- Support one-time and recurring tasks
- Manage large long-term tasks and personal projects

## Calendar and Events

- Monitor appointments, meetings, and important dates
- Track social meetings, dates, and plans with people
- Store birthdays, anniversaries, and other recurring events
- Send reminders before important dates
- Help plan upcoming weeks and months

## Health, Training, and Routines

- Track trainings, workouts, and physical activity
- Manage daily morning and evening routines
- Track supplements, stretching, skincare, hydration, meditation, and other habits
- Support recurring reminders for routines
- Monitor progress and consistency over time

## Hobbies and Personal Development

- Track hobbies and hobby-related tasks
- Manage learning goals, books, courses, projects, and creative activities
- Plan hobby sessions and long-term development goals
- Track progress in personal interests

## Planning and Long-Term Goals

- Manage big tasks and long-term plans
- Break large goals into smaller steps
- Track progress across multiple areas of life
- Support future plans, ideas, and “someday” tasks
- Organize goals related to home, health, work, finances, hobbies, and personal life

## Smart Reminders and Automation

- Generate reminders based on schedules, expiration dates, missing items, and overdue tasks
- Suggest shopping lists based on inventory and routines
- Highlight urgent, overdue, or upcoming actions
- Provide daily morning and evening overviews
- Help users stay organized across household, personal, and long-term responsibilities
